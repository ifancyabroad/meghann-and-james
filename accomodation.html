TRAVEL & ACCOMODATION

TRAVEL

Located roughly 30 minutes south from Barcelona, Sitges will be your base for the wedding!

The closest airport is Barcelona El Prat, flights arrive daily from the UK. 

We would advise hiring a car if you want to explore Sitges and the surrounding area.  Sitges can also be reached by train and bus from Barcelona Airport or you can grab a taxi.

Sitges is a classic Catalan coastal town flanked by sandy beaches and the Garraf National Park.  The town is very easily walkable with beaches, bars and restaurants on the doorstep of wherever you chose to stay.
